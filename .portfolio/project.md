Caret watches the text field you’re in and decides, in one model call, whether to stay quiet, finish your sentence or offer an action. A second call picks the workflow or computer task, and nothing runs until you press Tab to accept it. I designed that routing and wrote the Python core behind the Swift app. My team took first place at the Cursor x AITX hackathon.
